# Kohenoor Ecosystem Hub
## Alpha+ Beta Hardening Phase — Public App-by-App Summary
One unified ecosystem hub connecting 12 apps, intelligent learning, digital finance, and future-ready business control.

**Public release note:** This document is prepared as a safe public overview for GitHub and Zenodo sharing. The ecosystem is in Alpha+ beta hardening, with market-ready releases planned app by app during Q3 2026, subject to security testing, compliance review, infrastructure readiness, and user acceptance validation.

## App-by-App Public Summary
### 1. KEN-HyFi — Core / Settlements
KEN-HyFi is the settlement engine for real-world hybrid finance, connecting fiat-aware operations with blockchain-based transaction records and ecosystem payments. It delivers value to corporates through audit-ready settlement workflows and to individuals through clearer, safer digital finance participation.

### 2. KEN-Miner — DeFi / Mining
KEN-Miner is designed as an automated DeFi participation and reward-management layer for controlled ecosystem use cases. It helps corporates explore transparent tokenized reward models while giving individuals a guided interface for understanding staking, mining, and DeFi participation risks.

### 3. KENFI — Intelligence / Advisory AI
KENFI is the financial intelligence and advisory layer for trading, portfolio analysis, risk review, and decision-support insights. It supports corporates with structured investment intelligence and supports individuals with clearer market interpretation, planning discipline, and risk-aware financial education.

### 4. KAI — Intelligence / AI-BI
KAI is the advanced AI and business intelligence command center for governance, finance, operations, reporting, and institutional decision support. It gives corporates a controlled intelligence layer for coordination and analysis while giving individuals a smarter assistant for business, learning, and financial reasoning.

### 5. ProEdge — Utility / Education
ProEdge is the Education 3.0 training and skill development portal for AI, BI, blockchain, cybersecurity, hybrid finance, and future-ready professional capability. It helps corporates upskill teams and helps individuals gain practical, market-relevant competencies for the AI-powered economy.

### 6. KENCOM — Utility / Marketplace
KENCOM is the commerce layer of the KEN digital economy, enabling marketplace-style buying, selling, procurement, and service discovery. It creates value for corporates through digital trade channels and for individuals through simpler access to ecosystem products, services, and opportunities.

### 7. PowerKEN — DeFi / Investment
PowerKEN is the investment and wealth-acceleration interface for structured opportunity discovery, portfolio planning, and ecosystem participation. It helps corporates evaluate capital allocation pathways and helps individuals understand long-term planning, diversification, and responsible participation.

### 8. KEN Wallet — Core / Wallet Automation
KEN Wallet is a multi-currency wallet interface with built-in smart automation for ecosystem transactions, token access, and user-controlled digital asset activity. It gives corporates a secure transaction-access layer and gives individuals a simpler gateway for holding, using, and interacting with ecosystem assets.

### 9. Sentinel — Security / Surveillance
Sentinel is the financial and physical security-survival protocol layer for monitoring, alerts, continuity planning, and risk response. It helps corporates strengthen operational resilience and helps individuals understand risk signals, safety planning, and protective digital routines.

### 10. DevCon — Developer / Web3
DevCon is the smart contract lifecycle assistant for planning, developing, auditing, deploying, testing, and verifying token systems across RWA, CBDC pilots, meme, utility, and security-sensitive token classes. It helps corporates reduce launch risk and helps builders avoid weak architecture, hidden vulnerabilities, and poor verification practices.

### 11. PromptEx — Intelligence / AI Utility
PromptEx generates expert-level prompts, project structures, model-routing logic, and self-audit instructions for coding platforms and AI builders. It helps corporates accelerate controlled development workflows and helps individuals turn ideas into structured, testable, and customizable app plans.

### 12. PixilPro — Utility / Creative Design
PixilPro is the professional image editing and enhancement tool for creative, branding, forensic-support, CCTV enhancement, and visual intelligence use cases. It helps corporates improve visual documentation and campaign assets while giving individuals accessible AI-powered design and enhancement capability.
